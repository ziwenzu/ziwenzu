# Gary Ziwen Zu

**Assistant Professor of Political Science**  
University of Tennessee, Knoxville

[Website](https://ziwenzu.com/) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4852-4415) · [Email](mailto:zzu@utk.edu)

I study how states govern through law, information, and markets, with a focus on China and East Asia.

### Research

- **Law and politics** — Legal aid, legal mobilization, and the role of legal professionals under authoritarian rule.
- **Institutions and governance** — Administrative enforcement, state–society relations, and information control.
- **Political economy** — U.S.–China competition, trade, public procurement, and public opinion.

### Methods

My work combines field, audit, and survey experiments with causal inference and computational text and audio analysis.
